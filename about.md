## What this site is

A 12-month experiment in building a disciplined, public record of market thinking:

- **Weekly notes** on the markets I follow and the geopolitics behind them.
- **A paper-traded book**, with every idea written down before the outcome is known.
- **Monthly reviews** of what worked, what didn't, and why.

## How I use AI

I use Claude as a research assistant, not a decision-maker:

- **Automated:** a Python script pulls prices every Monday and updates the dashboard.
- **AI-assisted:** summarising data releases and news, drafting the "what happened" section,
  and arguing the other side of my trade ideas before I commit.
- **Mine alone:** the market views, trade decisions, sizing, and the final wording of each note.
